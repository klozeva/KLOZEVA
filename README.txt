# KLOZEVA Master Prototype

## Included
- One responsive KLOZEVA website with five modules.
- Beauty, Fashion, Salon and Fitness link to the four supplied demonstration URLs.
- Jewellery module plays the supplied MP4 video locally.
- QR code is generated on the live site and its Download QR button exports a PNG pointing to the site's deployed URL.
- No Lovable or Gemini builder branding is added to the site.

## Publish quickly with Netlify Drop
1. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag the entire `klozeva-master-prototype` folder onto the page (sign in/create a free account if prompted).
3. Netlify will publish it and give you a public URL.
4. Open that public URL. Scroll to "Take KLOZEVA with you" and click "Download QR · PNG".
5. Use that PNG on your INEX A0 poster.

Important: the four supplied demonstration links open as external demos in a new tab. The Jewellery MP4 is included in `assets/`.
